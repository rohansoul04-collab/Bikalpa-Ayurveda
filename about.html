Our Story

Discover the heart behind Bikalpa Ayurveda Clinic. We are a team of passionate healers dedicated to bringing balance, vitality, and true wellness to the community of Dhangadhi.

5+ Years

Healing Dhangadhi through Ancient Wisdom

Bikalpa Ayurveda Clinic was founded with a profound belief that nature, when properly understood, holds the cure to every ailment. Located in the heart of Dhangadhi, our clinic was founded with a singular purpose: to provide authentic, highly effective, and deeply compassionate Ayurvedic care to our local community.

We go beyond merely treating symptoms. By utilizing classical diagnostic methods like Nadi Pariksha (pulse diagnosis), we uncover the root causes of illness. Whether it is chronic joint pain, digestive issues, skin conditions, or lifestyle stress, our personalized treatments target the imbalance of your Doshas (Vata, Pitta, and Kapha).

"Ayurveda is not just a medical system; it is a way of life that teaches us how to maintain health and harmony."
